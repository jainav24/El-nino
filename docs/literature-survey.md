# Literature Survey

Traditional agricultural insurance models heavily utilize Generalized Linear Models (GLMs) to calculate flat premiums. However, research into the El Niño-Southern Oscillation (ENSO) indicates that climate risk is non-stationary and compounds over time.

Recent advances in predictive analytics suggest that **Survival Analysis**, originally utilized in medical research to track patient lifespans, provides superior risk estimation for temporal events such as crop failures, as it effectively models the *time-to-event* while handling right-censored observations (policies where no claim occurs).
